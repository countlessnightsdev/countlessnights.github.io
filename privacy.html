Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Dice A Million, developed by countlessnights.

Information Collection

Dice A Million does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The game does not require user accounts and does not request or collect information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information.

Analytics and Advertising

Dice A Million does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Third-party marketing tools

No gameplay or usage data is transmitted to the developer.

Save Data

Game progress and save data are stored locally on your device using the standard storage mechanisms provided by your operating system.

The developer does not have access to your save data.

Third-Party Services

Dice A Million does not integrate third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Dice A Million does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
